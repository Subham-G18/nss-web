National Service Scheme

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a public service program under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India.

Its motto is "Not Me, But You", reflecting the spirit of selfless service.

Objectives

  • Develop social responsibility among students
  • Promote community service and awareness
  • Encourage leadership and teamwork
